Who are you in this match?

Pick your name so "Mine" shows your highlights and your stats appear in the library. Saved for all demos.

Before recording
  • Close CS2 if it is running.
  • Close the FACEIT client (and its anti-cheat) from the tray. It must not run while HLAE is attached to the game.
  • CS2 opens in a window and records 1 highlight(s). Don't touch the game until it closes itself (about a minute per highlight).
  • Clips are saved in the app and copied to your Downloads folder.
Squad montage

One video of the best plays of you and your friends in this match. Clips that are not recorded yet are recorded first (CS2 opens and closes by itself), then everything is joined in match order. It is saved in the Highlights tab and your Downloads folder.

Steam ID is optional: without it the in-game name is matched (capitals and symbols ignored).
